Five marketplaces. One set of hands.

Whether your orders are flowing into Amazon Seller Central, exploding from a TikTok live, or trickling out of an Etsy shop, the work behind the scenes looks the same — careful, traceable, and on time. Here's exactly what we do, channel by channel.

01 Amazon FBA
Amazon Seller Central

FBA prep that never gets your shipment flagged.

The single biggest cost in Amazon FBA isn't the prep itself — it's the mistakes. Mislabeled cartons, missing poly bags, suffocation warnings on the wrong SKUs. Our team handles every shipment as if our seller account depended on it, because for our clients, it does.

  • FNSKU labeling on every unit, every time
  • Poly bagging with suffocation warnings
  • Bubble wrapping for fragile items
  • Expiration date and lot tracking
  • Bundling, kitting, and multi-pack creation
  • Shipment plan creation in Seller Central
  • Box content and 2D barcode prep
  • Carrier-ready palletizing

02 Amazon FBM
Merchant Fulfilled

FBM fulfillment with your name on the box.

Sometimes the math works out better outside FBA — oversized items, fast-moving local inventory, products that don't fit Amazon's prep matrix. We pick, pack, and ship FBM orders the same day they come in, at carrier rates we pass through without markup.

  • Same-day order cutoffs (when received before noon)
  • Order import from Amazon Seller Central
  • USPS, UPS, FedEx label generation
  • Carrier rates passed through at cost
  • Custom packaging and branded inserts
  • Tracking numbers synced back to Amazon
  • Real-time inventory reporting
  • Return processing and reintegration
