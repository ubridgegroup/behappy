01 / 05 Amazon Seller Central · FBA

FBA prep that never gets your shipment flagged.

The single biggest cost in Amazon FBA isn't the prep — it's the mistakes. Mislabeled cartons, missing poly bags, suffocation warnings on the wrong SKU. We handle every shipment as if our seller account depended on it, because for our clients, it does.

What's included

  • FNSKU labeling on every unit — applied, not stickered over
  • Poly bagging with compliant suffocation warnings (≥1.5 mil)
  • Bubble wrapping for fragile and glass items
  • Expiration date and lot tracking on every consumable
  • Bundling, kitting, and multi-pack creation
  • Shipment plan creation and split-shipment optimization
  • Box content labels and 2D barcode prep
  • Carrier-ready palletizing for LTL and SPD

How a FBA shipment moves

  1. 01

    Inbound received

    Photographed, counted, and matched against the PO. Any damage flagged before opening.

  2. 02

    Prep to spec

    FNSKUs printed, units bagged or wrapped per category, expiration dates logged.

  3. 03

    Shipment plan built

    Splits, box contents, and pallet labels generated in Seller Central — sent for your approval.

  4. 04

    Picked up by carrier

    Tracking numbers and photo proof of pack delivered before the truck leaves.

02 / 05 Amazon · Merchant Fulfilled
