About

Small enough to care. Built to scale with you.

Be Happy Prep Center is a boutique Amazon FBA and FBM prep facility tucked into Dania Beach, Florida. We started the way most good prep centers do — because the founders were sellers, and the prep centers they tried weren't good enough.

Our story

A prep center built by people who've shipped on Amazon themselves.

We've been on the other side of the dashboard. We know what it feels like to refresh Seller Central at midnight because a shipment was flagged. We know the cost of a single mislabeled carton, the late-night Slack messages, the supplier who promised one thing and delivered another.

So we built the prep center we wished we'd had: small enough that every shipment passes through the same careful hands, big enough to scale when a SKU takes off, and transparent enough that nothing about the work — including the price — is ever a surprise.

Today we're trusted by sellers across four major marketplaces, from boutique Etsy shops to seven-figure Amazon brands. We pride ourselves on being the partner you don't have to think about — the one that just does what it said it would, on time.

How we work

Four principles. No theatrics.

We don't post buzzword pages on how to run a warehouse. We just try to follow four things, every shipment, every day.

Precision over speed

A fast shipment with the wrong label is still a problem. We move quickly, but never at the cost of accuracy. The cheapest prep is the one you don't have to fix.

Transparent always

You'll see every line item before we charge it. Pricing lives publicly on this site. Surprises don't belong in a healthy business relationship.

Built to scale

40 units a day or 4,000 — the workflow doesn't change. We can add hands to a SKU within hours when a viral video or holiday spike hits.

Answer the phone

Real humans pick up. Email replies come the same day. The biggest difference between a good prep center and a great one is responsiveness.

Our philosophy

"Run the warehouse the way you'd want your inventory handled. Then keep doing that, even when nobody's watching."
— Operating principle, Day 1
