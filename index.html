How it works

Four steps, zero guesswork.

We're not a black box. Every shipment is logged, photographed, and tracked through a workflow we'll walk you through before your first box arrives.

01

You request a quote

Tell us SKU count, volume, and marketplaces. We respond within one business day with transparent per-unit pricing.

02

We receive your inventory

Send it from your supplier or 3PL. We check it in by box, pallet, or container — and photograph anything unusual.

03

We prep to spec

Label, bundle, bag, box, inspect. Your prep is done to the exact requirements of Amazon, TikTok, or your own brand.

04

It ships out clean

To FBA, to your buyer, or to your next destination. We send tracking, photos, and a clean record of every unit.
